# Prototype Instructions

- This folder is a self-contained, customer-facing prototype for a salon after-sales assurance library.
- Keep the experience English-first with a complete Chinese language switch for internal review.
- Policy details must distinguish receiving inspection, post-installation adjustment, and in-wear quality review windows.
- Never present one blanket warranty period across unrelated issues.
- Keep brand/product responsibility, salon installation responsibility, and end-customer care responsibility visibly separate.
- Use fictional order details only. Do not connect this prototype to production APIs or store submitted claim data.
- Treat policy windows and outcomes as a reviewable proposal until commercial, quality, and legal owners approve publication.
- Keep prototype styling self-contained with `--ls-*` tokens; do not import production frontend styles into this removable requirement folder.
- The production Vue single-file 500-line split rule does not apply to the standalone policy dataset or prototype stylesheet.
