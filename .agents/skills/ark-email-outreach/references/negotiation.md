# Price-response guardrails

Use this reference only after a customer raises price or commercial terms. Every price, discount, MOQ, payment term, inventory reservation, warranty, service, shipping offer, and delivery commitment requires current internal approval.

## Safe strategy families

The supplied course groups price responses into these families:

- clarify and reinforce differentiated value;
- use approved customer evidence;
- explain the factual reason for a price change;
- trade a concession for a confirmed reciprocal commitment;
- exchange price for volume, term, scope, service, or schedule;
- present approved tiered packages or two real options;
- bundle approved products/services;
- offer approved payment timing;
- link terms to an approved longer relationship;
- add approved services instead of reducing price;
- concede in approved stages;
- use current market benchmarks or transparent cost drivers;
- defer an approved benefit to a future order;
- reserve inventory under an approved agreement;
- start with an approved trial and revisit bulk pricing.

## Prohibited behavior

Do not:

- invent a CEO approval process or imply personal effort that did not happen;
- claim false scarcity, demand, stock, cost, benchmark, certification, or competitor weakness;
- fabricate named customer results;
- promise a discount after receiving a commitment unless the concession is already authorized;
- use manipulative pressure or hide material trade-offs;
- expose internal margins, costs, approval chains, or confidential customer details without authorization.

## Reply workflow

1. Extract the customer's actual objection and requested terms.
2. List every commercial commitment the proposed reply would make.
3. Match each commitment to an explicit internal approval or mark it pending.
4. Offer only approved options and state relevant conditions plainly.
5. Keep the tone collaborative and preserve the customer's ability to decline.
6. Show the exact reply and commitments to the user.
7. Use `outreach-queue preview` for the exact reviewed reply, then give the trusted operator the exact `confirm` command to run outside the Agent; never let the Agent invoke `confirm`, `agently-cli`, or the dispatcher directly.
