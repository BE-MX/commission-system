## 5. Claims that always require current review

Never approve from this snapshot alone:

- current inventory, production slot, lead time, shipment or arrival date;
- price, MOQ, discount, tier, rebate, margin, tax or shipping cost;
- free sample, sample refund/credit, intention fee, deposit or payment term;
- exclusivity, territory, shade protection, NDA scope or intellectual-property outcome;
- certification validity, certificate scope or destination compliance;
- product lifespan, “single donor/virgin for all colors”, zero tangling/shedding, or guaranteed performance;
- “fastest”, “best”, top ranking, competitor inferiority or scarcity;
- named customer, customer result, 2.5–3x margin, 20–30% price lift, reorder frequency or revenue result;
- unconditional refund, replacement, local return, compensation or problem-free promise;
- any statistic, macro prediction or performance number heard in training.

## 6. Known knowledge conflicts

- Company-history wording varies among “since 1993”, “more than 14 years”, “around 30 years family experience”, and “33 years”. Prefer “manufacturing since 1993” after confirming the current published fact.
- Some sales scripts present exclusivity, same-day shipping, 24-hour dispatch, 2–4-day arrival, free/refunded samples, product lifespan and customer margin as universal. Policy and dynamic-data documents restrict these claims.
- Document 380 is titled “需确认” and contains “fastest-delivery supplier” and other strong openings. Use its warm three-part structure only; do not reuse unsupported claims.

When sources conflict, use the narrower, more recent, explicitly governed policy; otherwise drop the claim and show the conflict to the reviewer.
