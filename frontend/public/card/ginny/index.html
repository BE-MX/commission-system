Hair Quality & Sourcing

Mainly Southwest China — untreated ponytail hair selected before industrial processing. Through long-term sourcing relationships, donors avoid perming, dyeing and bleaching before collection, giving us clean material for cuticle alignment and colour work.

Yes — 100% human hair with cuticle direction aligned root-to-tip. Reversed or uncertain hair is removed: a 10 kg finished order can need 12 kg+ of input, and rejected material is never mixed back in.

Standard products are mixed-donor, carefully sorted and controlled to one approved specification and master sample. We only describe hair as single-donor when it is separately collected and documented — never as a marketing claim.

No strong acid baths. Colour is processed slowly at 25–30°C — about 3 days for dark shades and 7–10 days for blonde.

No heavy silicone for temporary softness. After colouring we apply a professional silicone-free nourishing treatment to restore moisture.
